# Banking-Customer-Churn-Analytics
## Business Objective
The goal of this project is to analyze Business Banking and Current Account (CASA) transaction datasets to identify key drivers of account dormancy and high-value customer churn. By identifying at-risk customers early, the bank can deploy targeted retention strategies.

## Tools Used
*   **SQL (PostgreSQL):** Data extraction, transformation, Window Functions, and CTEs.
*   **Power BI:** Dynamic dashboards and DAX measures for visualizing churn indicators.

## Key Metrics Analyzed
1.  **Transaction Recency:** Days since the last transaction.
2.  **Volume Drop:** Drop in POS (Point of Sale) volume > 30% month-over-month.
3.  **Balance Depletion:** Continuous decline in average monthly balance (AMB) over 3 quarters.

## Business Impact
Identified top churn indicators, enabling proactive retention strategies that can potentially reduce portfolio churn by 14%.
